Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Information We Collect

This policy explains what information Harubaku Coffee's rewards app collects, how it's used, and what happens if you delete your account.

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely — we never store or have access to your actual password, only a securely hashed version)

We do not collect your phone number, location, or any marketing preferences.

Purchase and Rewards Activity

To operate the loyalty program, we store your stamp and rewards activity, including:

  • Stamps earned and free coffees redeemed
  • Transaction details such as date, receipt number, and which staff member processed it
  • Any messages you send us through the app's support feature

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Operate your account and rewards balance
  • Send you a password-reset email if you request one
  • Respond to support messages you send us

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing.

Third Parties

We use the following third-party service:

  • Zoho (email delivery) — used only to send password-reset emails. Zoho receives your email address for this purpose only. It never receives your name, password, or account/rewards data.

Our hosting provider, like any web host, automatically logs standard technical information (such as IP address, timestamp, and page requested) as part of normal operation. This is standard infrastructure-level logging, not something we configure or use ourselves.

We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts of any kind. Your session information is held only in your device's memory while you use the app and is cleared when you close or reload it — nothing is stored on your device.

Deleting Your Account

If you delete your account, we permanently remove your login credentials and contact information (your name, email, and password are erased or replaced so they can no longer identify you).

Your past purchase and redemption history is retained in anonymized form — it's no longer linked to your name or email — so we can maintain accurate business and accounting records. This anonymized history is not restored to any account and cannot be used to identify you.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@sipandfare.com.
