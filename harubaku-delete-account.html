Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 8, 2026

How to request account deletion

You can delete your account directly from the app:

  1. Open the Harubaku Rewards app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion

If you're unable to access the app, you can request deletion by emailing us at support@sipandfare.com.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove your login credentials and contact information — your name, email address, and password are erased or replaced so they can no longer identify you.

What we keep

Your past purchase and redemption history is retained in anonymized form — it's no longer linked to your name or email — so we can maintain accurate business and accounting records. This anonymized history cannot be used to identify you and is not restored to any account.

Retention period

There is no additional retention period. Your personal information (name, email, login credentials) is removed immediately upon deletion. Anonymized transaction history is retained indefinitely for business records, as described above.

Questions

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at support@sipandfare.com.
